I’m an AI Engineer on the humanoid team at NEURA Robotics and a Master’s student in Data Science at EPFL. Before joining NEURA, I was a Research Intern at VILAB with Amir Zamir, building vision tokenizers for 3D understanding.

I graduated in the top 3% of Spain’s first Artificial Intelligence cohort at UAB, did research at the Computer Vision Center, and wrote my BSc thesis at TUM with Daniel Cremers. I have publications at ECCV and ICDAR, and I want to advance physical AI and multimodal learning.
